PostgreSQL is the system of record, reached through SQLAlchemy 2.0 models (`backend/app/models/`) and repositories (`backend/app/repositories/`). Schema in `riskly_db.sql`: users, domain, domain_metric, model, analysis, api_log, traffic_history, reputation_event, acquisition_result, warmup_file. Sessions come from `core/database.py` (`get_db` commits on success / rolls back on error).
