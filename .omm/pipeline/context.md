The two models are asymmetric: the risk model always runs; the warm-up model runs only when a warm-up CSV was supplied, and its absence is modeled as "email not evaluated" (the base score is renormalized on authority alone) rather than a zero that would make the buy threshold unreachable. Feature engineering is the fragility point — TLD/country one-hot lists and weekday/month categories are pinned to fixed training values so inference codes match training.
