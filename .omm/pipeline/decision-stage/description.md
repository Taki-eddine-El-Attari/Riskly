`DecisionMatrix.decide`: the fusion rulebook. Normalizes rank (0–10 scale), backlinks (log scale to 1M), and age (to 10 years) into an authority score (weights 0.5/0.3/0.2); blends authority with email-health (0.5/0.5, or authority-only when email is missing); applies the malicious veto; then thresholds the security-discounted final score into WORTH_BUYING (≥0.60) / RISKY (≥0.30) / NOT_WORTH_IT, with INSUFFICIENT_DATA when no external signal exists. Marked as the risk node — all business thresholds live here.
